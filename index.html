Ordering & payment

The questions buyers usually ask before the first order.

05 / FAQ
What's the minimum order quantity?+
No fixed MOQ on trial orders — we'll quote whatever quantity you need to test a SKU or a new market, then scale up once it proves out.
How long does a sample take?+
7–15 days from a confirmed spec, depending on whether new tooling needs to be cut or an existing mold matches your drawing.
What payment methods do you accept?+
T/T bank transfer and L/C are both supported. Terms are confirmed per order, typically a deposit with balance before shipment.
Can you produce to my own drawing or match a competitor's part?+
Yes — this is the core of our OEM/ODM service. Send a CAD drawing, a physical sample, or a photo with dimensions and we'll quote tooling and lead time. See the full process on the Factory & OEM page.
Do you provide certificates with the shipment?+
Copies of our CE, EN 1935, EN 12209 and EN 1125 certificates are available on request with any quote.
